AI vs USA: How AI is Changing Peoples Relation in America

From friendships to work culture - AI is not replacing Americans, it is reshaping how Americans connect, work, and trust each other.

HUMAN CONNECTION

1. Americans are using AI as a Relationship Assistant

In USA, 68% of Gen-Z uses AI to write messages, fix fights, and plan dates. AI is becoming a third person in relationships - not to replace love, but to improve communication.

WORK & TRUST

2. Trust is Shifting in USA

Americans now trust AI for medical advice, job interviews, and even parenting tips. But the human trust - family, church, community - is still #1 in USA.
