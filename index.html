ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Useful AI comparisons, with the caveats included.

AI VS is an independent research starting point for people comparing AI software and planning US-focused content. We started this project because most "vs" pages hide pricing changes, plan limits, and real workflow differences.

What we do: We track 15 categories of AI tools (from chatbots and image generators to coding assistants and voice tools) and create 150 low-competition US keyword ideas. For each idea like "ChatGPT vs Claude for research" we provide search volume estimates, difficulty, what to verify in pricing, pros/cons, and a quick intent-led verdict.

How we research: We check official vendor pricing pages for US, test the free tier where possible, and validate SERPs manually. We do not claim to have tested every tool for 3 days. We provide a starting point and tell you exactly what to verify before you buy.

Who is behind it: Written by Ahmad, AI Tools Researcher based in Pakistan, focused on US market intent. This is a one-person independent project, not owned by any AI company. Contact: ai.vs.usa.contact@gmail.com
